## Elevator pitch

A 3D Pong game with personality. 1P vs five AI difficulty levels or 2P local. Every match you play against the AI is recorded — wins **and** losses — in a combined Hall of Fame / Hall of Shame. Losing to the easiest AI earns a permanent badge of shame. Beating the hardest earns bragging rights.

Ships as a single Docker container with an embedded SQLite database. Self-host in minutes; back up by copying one file.
